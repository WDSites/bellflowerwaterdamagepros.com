Before You Call

  • If it is safe, stop the water at the fixture or the main shutoff.
  • Stay away from electrical equipment standing in or near water.
  • Take photos of the damage before cleanup.
  • Have your address, ZIP code and a callback number ready.
  • Have your insurer's claim number if you already opened one.

What Happens After You Call

The dispatcher explains the first steps you can take and lines up a crew. Same-day response is offered when crews are available, and no arrival time is promised.

On site, the crew finds the wet areas, explains the scope and price and then starts work. See water mitigation for what the first visit looks like.

Non-Emergency Questions

For questions about a past job, a document request or a correction to something on this site, send an email. We read it during business days and reply as soon as we can. If the matter is urgent, call.
