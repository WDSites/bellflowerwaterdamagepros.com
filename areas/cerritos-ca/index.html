Start With the City Utility, Then Call Us

The City of Cerritos runs its own water utility with about 15,350 service connections, including wells, reservoirs, pipes and fire hydrants. In 2024 three deep wells supplied all of the city's drinking water. During business hours the Water Division is at (562) 407-2674. After hours, weekends and holidays the number is (562) 860-4018, which the city also uses for leaks in city streets.

If water is coming from a main or a street leak, the city handles the pipe. If it is inside your building, our line handles the water, the drying and the repair. Calling both is normal for a service line break.

A Planned City With High Ownership

Cerritos incorporated in 1956 as Dairy Valley and took its present name in 1967. Voters approved large-scale residential development in a 1963 special election. The 2020 census counted 16,374 housing units, and 76.4 percent of occupied units were owner occupied, well above Bellflower's 39 percent.

Homeowners here deal with their insurers directly, so ask about water damage and flood coverage before you need it, and document any loss with photos and a written scope.
