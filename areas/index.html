Bellflower and Home Base

Bellflower, ZIP code 90706, is where the dispatch address is, on Artesia Boulevard. The city borders Downey to the north and northwest, Norwalk and Cerritos to the east, Lakewood to the south and Paramount to the west. The San Gabriel River Freeway (I-605) is the east edge, the Artesia Freeway (SR 91) crosses the south and the Century Freeway (I-105) sits to the north.

The City of Bellflower is served by four water companies: Bellflower Home Garden Water Company, Bellflower-Somerset Mutual Water Company, California American Water and Liberty Utilities. The city's Public Works line at (562) 804-1424 can tell you which one serves an address.

The Five Nearby Towns

Downey is the largest, about three miles north, covering ZIP codes 90240, 90241 and 90242. Lakewood, about three miles south, covers 90712, 90713 and 90715 and is a planned community from the early 1950s.

Paramount, about three miles west, covers 90723. Norwalk, about four miles east across the I-605, covers 90650. Cerritos, about five miles southeast, covers 90703 and 90701. Distances are rough, straight-line estimates.

Safe First Steps in Any Town

  • If water is near outlets, appliances or a panel, stay out and leave the power off until it is checked.
  • Shut off the water at the fixture or the main valve if you can reach it safely.
  • Move people, pets and small valuables out of the wet area.
  • Photograph the damage before anything is moved.
  • Call 911 first for fire, gas odor or any risk to life.
