Sewage Backup: What to Do First

People searching for this usually say the drain is backing up or the floor is covered in dirty water. That is a Category 3 situation, which means the water is grossly contaminated and should not be touched. Stay out of the room, keep children and pets away and do not run more water down the drains.

Call the 24/7 line. A crew uses protective equipment, extracts the water, removes porous materials that cannot be cleaned and sanitizes the area. If the backup is in the city's sewer line, the sewer utility must be notified, and the dispatcher can tell you who to call.

When Flooding Is an Emergency

Flooding is an emergency when water is entering the building, when it came from outside or from a drain, or when it covers a large area of floor. The dispatch line answers at night and on weekends, and same-day response is offered when crews are available. No arrival time is promised.

While you wait, avoid contact with the water, turn off power at the panel only if you can reach it on dry ground and move valuables up. Leave the area and call 911 for any electrical or gas risk. Take photos from the doorway before anything is moved.

Basement Flooding Cleanup

Basements are uncommon in many Southern California homes, so this section applies to the ones that have them and to lower-level rooms, below-grade garages and parking areas in multi-unit buildings. Water that comes in from outside or from a floor drain is treated as contaminated until proven otherwise.

The crew pumps the water out, removes wet drywall, carpet and insulation that cannot be cleaned, sanitizes and dries the space. Restoration does not stop water from coming back. If groundwater or drainage is the cause, a drainage or waterproofing contractor handles that side, and we will say so on the call.

Flood Insurance and Claims

The California Department of Insurance says standard homeowners policies generally exclude flood damage. Flood coverage is bought separately, usually through the National Flood Insurance Program, and a new policy typically takes effect 30 days after purchase. Renters need their own flood coverage too if they want it.

Some drain backups are covered by a policy endorsement, and others are not. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and document everything with photos before cleanup. We provide readings and a written scope, but we do not promise a claim will be approved.

Cost Factors

Flood restoration pricing follows the water category, the area, the materials that must be removed, how many days of drying are needed and how much is rebuilt. Category 3 jobs cost more because of protective gear, containment and disposal.
