How to Use These Guides

Each guide answers one question people ask after water gets into a home. Most include a checklist, a comparison table or a short list of questions to ask before you hire anyone, and each ends with a local note about Bellflower or a nearby town.

They are general information. Your policy, your lease and your building decide the details, so use the guides to ask better questions and not to replace advice from your insurer, landlord or a physician.
